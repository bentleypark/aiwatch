# AIWatch Trademark Policy

## 1. What this covers

This policy covers the name **AIWatch**, the AIWatch wordmark and logo, and the official site at
**ai-watch.dev** (together, the "Marks"). They identify the AIWatch project at
[github.com/bentleypark/aiwatch](https://github.com/bentleypark/aiwatch).

The AIWatch source code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)). That licence lets you use, modify and redistribute the **code**. It does not
grant any right to use the **Marks**. This policy says what you may do with the Marks.

The principle behind every rule below: **do not mislead people about what they are using or who
runs it.**

## 2. Always allowed

You do not need permission to:

- Refer to AIWatch accurately: "we use AIWatch", "data from AIWatch", "compatible with AIWatch".
- Link to ai-watch.dev or to this repository, and use the official badges and feeds exactly as
  documented in the README.
- Run a copy for your own use or your organisation's internal use.
- Fork this repository on GitHub, keeping the repository name `aiwatch`, to develop changes or
  contribute them back.
- Say truthfully that your project is a fork of, or is based on, AIWatch.

## 3. Public deployments and modified versions

If you offer a copy of the software to the public as a service, whether or not you changed it, or you
distribute a modified version as a product:

1. **Do not present it as the official AIWatch.** Choose your own product name. You may describe
   the relationship in plain words: "[Your Name], based on AIWatch".
2. **Do not use the AIWatch logo or wordmark.** Use your own branding.
3. **Show a notice** where people use or download it, for example:
   "This is based on AIWatch (github.com/bentleypark/aiwatch). It is not operated, reviewed or
   endorsed by the AIWatch project."
4. **Keep your data separate.** Statuses, scores and rankings produced by your own deployment must
   not be presented as AIWatch's own readings or as the "AIWatch Score" published at ai-watch.dev.

## 4. Not allowed without written permission

- Using the Marks, or anything confusingly similar, as part of your own product, company, domain,
  app, social-media account or organisation name. "Confusingly similar" includes spelling
  variations, phonetic equivalents, translations and transliterations of the Marks.
- Using the logo or the look of the official site in a way that suggests yours is the official one.
- Suggesting that the project has reviewed, certified, sponsored or endorsed your product, service
  or data.
- Registering the Marks, or similar marks, as trademarks, domain names or handles.

## 5. Existing uses

If you are already using the Marks in a way section 3 or 4 does not allow, please update it promptly,
or contact us to agree a timeline.

## 6. Asking permission or reporting a problem

Questions, permission requests and reports of confusing use: **contact@ai-watch.dev**. Include the
URL and a short description. Silence is not permission.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this repository's default branch is the current one.
